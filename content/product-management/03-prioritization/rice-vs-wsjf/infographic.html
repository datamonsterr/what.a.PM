Two formulas, one idea: value ÷ effort

RICE Intercom

Reach × Impact × ConfidenceEffort
Reach
People or events per time period (e.g. users per quarter). A real count.
Impact
Per person: 3 massive · 2 high · 1 medium · 0.5 low · 0.25 minimal.
Confidence
100% high · 80% medium · 50% low. Discounts your own optimism.
Effort
Person-months across product, design and engineering.

Best for: comparing product ideas where reach can be counted.

WSJF SAFe

Cost of DelayJob Size
Cost of Delay =
User-business value + Time criticality + Risk reduction / opportunity enablement.
Time criticality
Does value drop if we wait? Deadlines, market windows, regulation.
Risk reduction / opportunity
Does doing it lower risk or unlock future work?
Job size
Relative size (a proxy for duration). Scored 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20 with the smallest item in each column set to 1.

Best for: sequencing larger features where timing and risk matter.

🧱 The ground underneath both: capacity is finite (FP-001), value is judged by customers (FP-002), and delay costs value (FP-004). WSJF makes delay explicit; RICE makes uncertainty explicit through Confidence.
